# PokemonAnte
I want to make a pokemon rom hack for Fire Red.  I'd like to base it on the concept of the old Magic The Gathering rule about Antes. My initial thoughts:

major distinctions:

You can't catch wild Pokémon (maybe not even encounter them)

When you fight a trainer, from your team of 6 you randomly draw 1 as your ante

winner of the battle gets the ante pokemon

trainers you beat enough times will start filling their set with random pokemon from the wild encounters table

If you lose to a trainer, they have your pokemon, and could even have it in their set the next time you fight

Considering: a store where you can buy "packs" of pokemon.

Think about this for a while, and help me decide how else we can work the rules around this.
